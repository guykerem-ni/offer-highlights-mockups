Reviewer guidance

What we want to evaluate with these examples

Use the mock-ups to pressure-test whether Offer Highlights can become a useful NI distribution surface, and what Microsoft would need from us to make the experience practical across categories.

Category fit

Which verticals feel natural in a Copilot answer, and where the pattern needs different language for subscription versus retail journeys.

Offer data model

Which fields NI should be able to provide reliably: offer copy, merchant details, price framing, eligibility cues, ratings, and supporting assets.

Sponsored treatment

Whether the sponsored row is clear, useful, and commercially meaningful without making the recommendation feel less trustworthy.

Partner conversation

What questions we should take to Microsoft around placement, contribution workflow, tracking, compliance, and update cadence.
