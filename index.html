What to evaluate

Whether the card, detail panel, and sponsored offer pattern communicates enough value without overloading the Copilot answer.

What is static

The pages are static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and local assets. There is no live feed, personalization, or backend integration in this package.

What travels

The same renderer supports retail and subscription modes, making it easier to discuss a common data contract across verticals.
